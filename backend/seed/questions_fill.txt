# Fill-in-the-blank questions.
#   @id fill topic
#   <lang> sentence with exactly one [answer] | distractor, distractor, distractor
# Each language has its own blank and distractors (the grammar point differs per language);
# the sentences are translations of each other so the native sentence works as a hint.
# Distractors must be clearly wrong in the sentence, not just less likely.
# `why <target>.<native> <option> | <reason>` lines explain every option (see seed/es/README.md);
# needed for zh (in English) and for en/fr (in Chinese and English). The es lines here are never shown.
